      Final Score +0
      • 10×10 grid, single player.
      • 1 move per turn, orthogonal (no diagonals).
      • Players can share the same cell.
      • First turn must be onto the IN cell (1, 1).
      • Stepping on OUT (10, 10) advances everyone to the next level. Slides also count — sliding onto OUT exits the level just like a normal step.
      • Game over after 11 levels. Level 0 is a tutorial corridor (just walk to OUT); Level 1 is a warm-up with 6 spiders and no effects or items.
      • Effects fire when you enter the cell.
      • Slide chains into other effects, but stops on another slide. Starting your turn on a slide lets you pick the slide direction. Knight is a jump. Slides do not stop on OUT — your slide must come to rest on the OUT cell to exit.
      • Lava kills any player or spider that enters it. Pushing a rock onto a lava cell turns it into a permanent boulder.
      • TnT blows you up and resets the level. Triggering TnT with a rock or spider clears the cell (and earns you +10).
      • Spiders wander randomly each turn, but chase the closest player within 3 squares.
      • If a spider catches you, you die and restart at IN.
      • Treasure chests are either a Spider Knife (kills the next spider that catches you) or a Spider Freeze Potion (freezes spiders for 4 of your turns).
      • If every on-grid player is invisible, spiders stay put.
